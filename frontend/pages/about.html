Our story

A cafe built around a courtyard and a very good cup of coffee.

Why Grounds & Groves

Grounds are what is left after a perfect brew. Groves are where good things grow. We wanted a place that respects both: honest coffee, and a space that feels like a garden.

We buy beans from small estates, roast them in small batches every week, and bake our bread and cakes each morning. The menu is short on purpose, so everything on it is done well.

What we care about

  • Fresh, not fast. Orders are made when you ask for them.
  • Fair sourcing. We pay estates directly and know the people who grow our coffee.
  • Less waste. Coffee grounds go to the courtyard plants, and delivery packaging is compostable.
