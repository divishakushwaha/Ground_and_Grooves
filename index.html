Our Story

Grounds & Groove is a coffee bar and record shop under one roof — a place built for lingering, not rushing through a cup.

How It Started

It began in 2018, when two friends — one a barista, the other a record collector — took over a shuttered music store on Koramangala's 80 Feet Road. Neither wanted to give up their trade, so they kept both: the shop's old wooden shelving still holds vinyl on one wall, and a hand-built espresso bar runs along the other.

The name came from that split identity — "Grounds" for the coffee, "Groove" for the records — and it stuck the day a regular asked to hear side two of whatever was already spinning, before ordering.

How We Work Today

We still roast in small batches every Tuesday, sourcing beans from single estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg. Every table gets to hear whatever's on the turntable that hour, and regulars are welcome to bring in a record from home for the staff to queue up.
